# 2025 Federal Tax Rules Reference

## Key Changes for 2025 (OBBBA — One Big Beautiful Bill Act)

### Tax Brackets
- 7 brackets remain: 10%, 12%, 22%, 24%, 32%, 35%, 37%
- TCJA rates made **permanent** (were set to expire)
- Thresholds inflation-adjusted for 2025

### Standard Deduction
- Single: $15,750
- MFJ: $31,500
- HoH: $23,625
- Additional 65+: $2,000 (single) / $1,600 (married)
- Additional blind: $2,000 (single) / $1,600 (married)

### NEW: Schedule 1-A Deductions (even with standard deduction)
1. **Qualified Tips Exclusion**: Up to $25,000 for tips in traditionally tipped occupations. MAGI phase-out starts at $160,000.
2. **Overtime Premium Pay**: Up to $12,500 deduction for overtime pay above regular rate. MAGI phase-out starts at $160,000.
3. **Auto Loan Interest**: Interest on loans for US-assembled vehicles purchased after enactment. Vehicle price cap: $100,000.
4. **Senior Bonus Deduction**: $4,000 additional deduction for taxpayers 65+ taking standard deduction.

### SALT Deduction
- Cap raised to **$40,000** (from $10,000)
- MFS cap: $20,000
- Phase-out starts at AGI $400,000 (10% reduction per $20,000 over)
- Floor: $10,000

### Child Tax Credit
- Increased to **$2,500 per child** (from $2,000)
- Refundable portion: $1,700
- Phase-out: $200,000 single / $400,000 MFJ

### QBI Deduction
- Made **permanent** (was expiring after 2025)
- 20% of qualified business income
- Taxable income threshold: $197,300 single / $394,600 MFJ

## Self-Employment Tax
- Combined rate: 15.3% (12.4% SS + 2.9% Medicare)
- SS wage base: $176,100
- Additional Medicare: 0.9% above $200,000 single / $250,000 MFJ
- 50% of SE tax is deductible above-the-line

## Capital Gains
- Short-term: taxed as ordinary income
- Long-term rates: 0%, 15%, 20%
- NIIT: 3.8% on investment income above $200,000 single / $250,000 MFJ
- Net capital loss deduction: $3,000/year ($1,500 MFS)
- Excess losses carry forward indefinitely

## Crypto / Digital Assets (2025 Changes)
- **1099-DA**: Brokers must report gross proceeds for first time
- Cost basis reporting NOT required until 2026
- **Rev. Proc. 2024-28**: Cost basis tracked per wallet/account (not global pooling)
- Form 8949 new boxes: G/H/I (short-term digital), J/K/L (long-term digital)
- Wash sale rules: Currently no explicit rule for crypto (may change)
- Staking/airdrops/mining = ordinary income at FMV when received
