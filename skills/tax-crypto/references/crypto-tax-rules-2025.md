# Crypto / Digital Asset Tax Rules — 2025

## Taxable Events
1. **Selling crypto for fiat** — capital gain/loss
2. **Trading crypto for crypto** — capital gain/loss (each side is a disposal)
3. **Spending crypto on goods/services** — capital gain/loss
4. **Receiving crypto as income** — ordinary income at FMV

## Non-Taxable Events
1. Buying crypto with fiat
2. Transferring between own wallets (same owner)
3. Gifting (under annual exclusion; donee inherits basis)

## Income Classification
| Event Type | Tax Treatment | Reported On |
|---|---|---|
| Staking rewards | Ordinary income at FMV when received | Schedule 1 Line 8z |
| Airdrops | Ordinary income at FMV when received | Schedule 1 Line 8z |
| Mining (hobby) | Other income | Schedule 1 Line 8z |
| Mining (business) | Self-employment income | Schedule C |
| DeFi lending interest | Interest income | Schedule B |
| Hard fork (new coins) | Ordinary income at FMV when received | Schedule 1 Line 8z |

## Cost Basis Methods
- **FIFO** (First In, First Out) — default for Open Tax
- LIFO, HIFO, Specific ID also allowed but must be elected consistently
- Per Rev. Proc. 2024-28: basis tracked **per wallet/account**, not globally pooled

## Form 8949 Boxes (2025)
| Box | Meaning |
|---|---|
| G | Short-term digital asset, basis reported to IRS |
| H | Short-term digital asset, basis NOT reported to IRS |
| I | Short-term digital asset, no 1099 |
| J | Long-term digital asset, basis reported to IRS |
| K | Long-term digital asset, basis NOT reported to IRS |
| L | Long-term digital asset, no 1099 |

## 2025 Changes
- **1099-DA**: Brokers must report gross proceeds (first year)
- Cost basis reporting NOT required until 2026
- Wash sale rules: No explicit statutory rule for crypto yet (may change)
- DeFi protocol reporting: Not yet required but proposed
